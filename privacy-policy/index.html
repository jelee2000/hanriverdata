Privacy policy

Effective: October 15, 2026. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails for people in Korea.

Han River Data ("we") licenses Korean conversational audio, recorded with the consent of every speaker, as AI training data. This policy explains what personal information we handle and how.

We collected the contact details of shows we contact from public sources such as podcast pages. On request we will tell you the source and stop contacting you.

Before providing data, we remove direct identifiers such as names and contact details from filenames and file information and replace them with consistent speaker codes. Where we find direct identifiers in recordings or transcripts, we mask or exclude those passages as needed. Names, contact details, consent forms, and payment records are kept separately from the training data.

A voice can be personal information even after names are removed. We do not treat recordings as anonymous, we process them only as speakers agreed, and we never use voices to identify or authenticate anyone or create voiceprints.

2. Sensitive information

Speakers may mention their health, religion, political views, or similar topics. We process this only with separate consent; otherwise we remove the segment or exclude the episode.

3. Who receives it

  • The client named in your release (its name and country are stated in the release you sign) receives the recordings, transcripts, pseudonymous speaker codes, and optional attributes for AI training and evaluation. By contract, it may not clone voices, identify or re-identify speakers, republish recordings, or pass them on. If we add a client, we will tell speakers and ask for consent again.
  • Korean National Tax Service receives payee details as tax law requires.
  • Service providers process data only on our instructions: Google LLC (storage, email).

4. International transfer

We operate from New York and store data with Google LLC in the United States, so data is processed in the US. Recordings also go to the client named in your release, in the country stated there, after delivery and acceptance, by access-restricted cloud folder or secure transfer. Speakers can refuse overseas transfer in the release; their recordings are then left out.

5. Deletion

We delete data when its retention period ends or its purpose is met, using methods that cannot be recovered. Data we must keep by law is stored separately. Clients delete data within 30 days after the license period ends or after we ask, and certify deletion in writing.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing founders@hanriverdata.com. We will verify your identity and reply within 10 days. If you withdraw, we stop delivering your recordings, delete our copies, and ask clients to delete theirs; training already done cannot be reversed. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information; withdrawing consent does this.

7. Security

Access to original files is limited to people who need it; we never share files by public link and use accounts with two-step verification; delivered data carries pseudonymous speaker codes instead of names; the key linking names to codes, and tax IDs, are kept in separate restricted storage; and clients must notify us of any breach within 72 hours.

8. Cookies and tracking

We may use cookies and local storage only as needed for core functions such as running and securing the website, language settings, and remembering your consent choices. We do not currently use analytics or advertising and marketing tracking tools.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies may stop some features from working.

We never disclose voice recordings, spoken content, consent forms, or speakers' or providers' personal information for advertising or marketing.

We post changes on this page 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days before for significant changes. Earlier versions are available on request.
