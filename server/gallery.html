a few notes

charcoal
#252723
warm ivory
#f7f6f2
  • use the square mark for profile pictures. use the horizontal logo when the name needs to travel with it.
  • use charcoal on light backgrounds and ivory on dark backgrounds. transparent files have no background.
  • keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its height. the square and banner files already include this space.
  • keep the proportions, two riverbanks, and open channel intact. avoid stretching, rotation, outlines, gradients, and shadows.
  • use svg for crisp scaling and png for quick sharing. the logo lettering is outlined, so no font installation is needed.
